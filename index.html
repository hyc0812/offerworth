About this estimate

OfferWorth provides an annualized estimate for regular employment income using standard/basic tax-credit assumptions. Results may differ from an employer paycheque because payroll withholding can depend on pay dates, TD1 claims, pension or RRSP deductions, bonuses, commissions, benefits, union dues, and other circumstances.
